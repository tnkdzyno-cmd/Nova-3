Role permission matrix

What each role can do. Enforced server-side on every request — this table is a reference, not a control.
RoleCan
System AdministratorEverything, including granting auditor access.
Vice-ChancellorEverything except user administration — reverses journals, closes periods, approves AP payments/budgets, payroll import, sees Management Accounts (KPIs + approvals queue). Consolidates what used to be separate Finance Manager and Principal roles.
Accounts OfficerPosts day-to-day transactions (invoices, payments, journals). Cannot reverse journals, close periods, or reach payroll admin.
BursarStudent billing and receipting only.
AuditorRead-only, and only in the specific data domains a System Administrator has granted — not a blanket "sees everything" role. See a given auditor's granted access in the table above.
IT AdministratorUser directory, audit log, and grants/revokes auditor access. No financial data access itself.
Budget OfficerCreates and submits budgets. Cannot approve its own submissions — the Vice-Chancellor reviews and approves.
